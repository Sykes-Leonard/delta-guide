# Anthropic Safeguards & Alignment: Institutional Profile, Governance & Research Agenda

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Institutional Charter

Founded in 2021 by former research executives and scientists from OpenAI, **[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/)** is an AI safety and research company dedicated to building frontier artificial intelligence systems that are provably reliable, interpretable, and steerable. Headquartered in San Francisco, California, Anthropic is organized as a **Public Benefit Corporation (PBC)**.

* **Mission & Scope**: Anthropic was established under the premise that transformative AI systems pose profound catastrophic and societal risks unless safety and alignment research lead or parallel capability development. Its corporate charter legally requires its directors to balance commercial success with its public benefit mission: developing safe, beneficial AI that upholds human autonomy and well-being.
* **Genesis & Affiliation**: Governed through a unique institutional structure called the **Long-Term Benefit Trust (LTBT)**—an independent panel of five trustees with fiduciary responsibility for AI safety rather than shareholder returns—Anthropic actively collaborates with national safety institutes (US CAISI, UK AISI) and external academic labs.
* **Key Leadership & Fellows**: Led by **Dario Amodei** (Chief Executive Officer), **Daniela Amodei** (President), **Chris Olah** (Interpretability Research Lead), and **Jared Kaplan** (Chief Science Officer).

### Core Research & Policy Pillars:
* **Pillar 1: Constitutional AI (CAI)**: Aligning models via Reinforcement Learning from AI Feedback (RLAIF) using an explicit "constitution" derived from foundational human rights documents rather than opaque, subjective human preferences.
* **Pillar 2: Mechanistic Interpretability**: Pioneering "dictionary learning" and Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs) to decompose neural network representations into discrete, interpretable human concepts (**monosemanticity**), opening the "black box" of internal reasoning.
* **Pillar 3: Responsible Scaling Policies (RSP)**: Enforcing dynamic containment tiers (**AI Safety Levels / ASL-1 to ASL-4**) that tie compute scaling to verified containment, red-teaming thresholds, and cybersecurity guarantees against model weight exfiltration.
