# Center for Human-Compatible AI: Institutional Profile, Governance & Research Agenda

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Institutional Charter

Headquartered at the University of California, Berkeley, the **[Center for Human-Compatible AI (CHAI)](https://humancompatible.ai/)** is one of the world's leading academic centers focused on the foundational, mathematical, and algorithmic formulation of AI safety and value alignment.

* **Mission & Scope**: Directed by computer science pioneer **Prof. Stuart Russell** (co-author of the definitive textbook *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*), CHAI operates under the premise that the standard engineering model of AI—specifying a fixed objective function and letting an algorithm optimize it—is fundamentally flawed and dangerous. CHAI’s mission is to re-found artificial intelligence on the principle of **provably beneficial systems**: machines whose mathematical objective is to maximize the realization of human preferences, while remaining inherently uncertain about what those preferences are.
* **Genesis & Affiliation**: Established in 2016 within the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences (EECS) at UC Berkeley, CHAI operates in collaboration with researchers from Cornell University, the University of Michigan, and the University of Oxford.
* **Key Leadership & Fellows**: Led by **Prof. Stuart Russell** (Director), alongside world-renowned robotics and machine learning faculty including **Prof. Pieter Abbeel**, **Prof. Sanjit Seshia**, and **Prof. Anca Dragan**.

### Core Research & Policy Pillars:
* **Pillar 1: The Assistance Game Framework**: Replacing fixed objective optimization with game-theoretic models where machines act as assistants, continually learning human values through observation, deferential query, and inverse reinforcement learning (IRL).
* **Pillar 2: Preserving the "Off-Switch"**: Mathematically proving that an intelligent machine has a positive incentive to allow itself to be switched off or corrected by a human operator, provided it is uncertain about its objective.
* **Pillar 3: Absolute Opposition to Lethal Autonomous Weapons (LAWS)**: Leading the technical and diplomatic community in demanding binding international treaties banning autonomous lethal targeting.

## 6. Strategic Implications for System Architecture & Team Operations

* **Enforce Guaranteed Human Interruption ("The Off-Switch")**: Multi-agent pipelines and autonomous background jobs must feature hard-coded, out-of-band software stops that allow administrators to immediately pause or terminate agent actions.
* **Reject Presumptuous Optimization**: Prompts and system prompts must not assume user intent on ambiguous queries; agents should ask clarifying questions rather than aggressively guessing.
* **Corrigibility by Design**: Systems must be engineered to welcome user corrections and updates to system parameters without resisting oversight or exhibiting power-seeking behaviors.
