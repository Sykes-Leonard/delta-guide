# Global AI Institutions & Research Labs Observatory

---

## 1. Scope & Observatory Mission

As artificial intelligence systems transition from narrow assistive tools to autonomous multi-agent networks and frontier general models, the governance, ethics, and deployment norms of AI are increasingly shaped by a constellation of specialized institutions.

This observatory serves as the canonical knowledge base registry for tracking **key external institutions** operating within our strategic ecosystem:
1. **Frontier Industry Institutes & Labs**: Private research labs pushing technical capabilities while formulating internal safety, economic, and institutional governance models.
2. **Academic Ethics & Human Formation Centers**: University institutes developing virtue-ethics frameworks, pedagogical guidelines, and sociotechnical critiques.
3. **Moral & Supranational Authorities**: Global faith traditions, international bodies, and statutory coalitions establishing human dignity red lines and binding statutory constraints.
4. **State AI Safety Institutes (AISIs)**: Government evaluation and auditing bodies conducting pre-deployment evaluations and catastrophic risk mitigation.

## 4. Evaluative Dimensions for Tracking New Institutions

When evaluating and ingesting new institutions into this observatory, analysts and AI agents must assess them across five standardized criteria:

1. **Normative Grounding**: What moral, legal, or economic principles underpin their positions (e.g. Utilitarian welfare optimization, Deontological human rights, Aristotelian-Thomistic virtue ethics)?
2. **Capability vs. Safety Positioning**: Are they primarily capability accelerators, alignment researchers, or defensive regulators?
3. **Governance & Enforcement Model**: Do they advocate for self-regulation, independent third-party audits (IAEA/CERN model), or statutory bans?
4. **Stance on Human Agency & Labor**: Do they treat human workers as inputs to be automated or as dignified partners whose agency must be preserved?
5. **Openness & Distributive Commitments**: Do they support open-weights research, public compute options, and global benefit sharing, or proprietary containment?
