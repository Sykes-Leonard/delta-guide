# Harvard University: Institutional Overview of AI Research, Ethics & Pedagogy

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Institutional Scope

**Harvard University** approaches artificial intelligence not as a purely technical discipline, but as a transformative sociotechnical force requiring deep integration across moral philosophy, empirical social science, legal theory, and innovative pedagogy. 

Rather than centralizing its AI strategy under a single commercial or technical umbrella, Harvard deploys a distributed, cross-faculty model. The university’s work is characterized by two distinct, world-leading pillars:
1. **Empirical Human Flourishing & Ethical Measurement**: Led by the Institute for Quantitative Social Science (IQSS), assessing how algorithms impact mental health, character virtue, relational depth, and authentic human flourishing.
2. **Socratic AI Pedagogy & Cognitive Scaffolding**: Led by the Derek Bok Center and course leaders (notably CS50), engineering custom pedagogical AI systems designed to preserve student "productive struggle" through disciplined Socratic interaction.
