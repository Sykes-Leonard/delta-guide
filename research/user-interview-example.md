# Customer Discovery & Workflow Observations

## 1. Session Information
* **Role**: Operational Supervisor & Support Lead
* **Date**: 2026-09-26
* **Setting**: In-depth qualitative interview & screen share observation
* **Objective**: Identify manual bottlenecks in customer intake and task triage

---

## 2. Key Insights & Takeaways
1. **Manual Sorting Fatigue**: Operators spend 15–20% of their day manually reassigning tickets and routing requests.
2. **Lack of Priority Visibility**: Urgent requests frequently get buried behind older, non-critical routine queries.
3. **Desire for Smart Automation**: Staff expressed overwhelming enthusiasm for automated priority scoring if they retain the ability to manually override decisions.

---

## 3. Notable Quotes
> *"If an urgent request arrives during the morning rush, nobody notices until someone calls in angry. If the system could automatically bubble that to the top, it would save hours of fire-fighting."*
